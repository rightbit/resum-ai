# Leadership Experience

## Managing Distributed Teams

Ryan has managed distributed engineering teams spanning multiple time
zones and countries, including teams at DigiCert with members located
across North America and Europe. He has established practices for
asynchronous communication, documentation-first decision-making, and
equitable on-call rotations across time zones.

## Mentorship & Career Growth

Ryan has mentored engineers into senior and lead roles, and has
contributed to engineering career ladder definitions used for promotion
and performance conversations.

## Cross-Functional Leadership

Ryan regularly partners with product management, security, compliance,
and data science teams to align engineering roadmaps with business goals,
acting as a bridge between technical execution and business strategy.
