# Selected Projects

## ACME Certificate Automation Platform (DigiCert)

Led the engineering effort to build an ACME-protocol-compatible
certificate automation platform, allowing customers to automatically
issue and renew TLS certificates without manual intervention. This
project involved coordinating multiple engineering teams and integrating
with existing certificate authority infrastructure.

## Loan Matching Engine (Lendio)

Directed the rebuild of Lendio's loan matching engine, which evaluates
small-business loan applications against a marketplace of lending
partners and returns ranked matches. The project included close
collaboration with data science to improve match quality and with
product management to refine the borrower experience.

## Internal Developer Platform

Sponsored and helped design an internal developer platform effort aimed
at standardizing CI/CD pipelines, service scaffolding, and observability
tooling across multiple engineering teams, reducing the time to stand up
a new service from days to hours.
